About FreeStream TV Guide

A simple, independent directory for Android TV and Google TV viewers.

FreeStream TV Guide helps people discover streaming applications and useful TV utilities in one searchable place. Browse the directory by region, search by app name, and use the official store or developer links provided on each listing.

This website is an independent information directory. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any app developer, broadcaster, streaming service, or app store. App names and logos are the property of their respective owners.

Important: Listings and version numbers reflect the source collection and may no longer be current. Availability, pricing, free tiers, device support and catalogues can change. Always confirm details on the official listing before installing. Respect regional availability and each service's terms. This site does not host APK files or stream video content.
